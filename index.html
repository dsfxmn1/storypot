The Show

The Storypot is a fiction podcast where every story is written to be heard out loud, not read off a page. Short fiction written, voiced, and produced by hand by Marco Nieves in a small studio with a big red light. Warm narrators, characters who talk like people, and sound design that paints what the camera can't.

· · ·

What You'll Hear

Fractured

Stories from the world of the Fractured Realities novels: three lives caught in the same undertow, each holding onto what the world keeps trying to take from them. If the episode stays with you, the books go deeper.

The Moments Before

An anthology of final moments. What would you do if you knew the end was coming? Not a vague someday. Right now. In the next hour. Six voices, six last hours, one question.

Voices in the Smoke

A multi-part story told from the wreckage of a city in crisis. Two survivors, eleven floors through a blacked-out stairwell, and a night that keeps going after you think you've made it out.

A new story arrives every month.
